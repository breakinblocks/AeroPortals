# AeroPortals 1.2.0

## Added

- The teleport command now takes optional landing coordinates: `/aeroportals teleport <dimension> [x y z]`, for example `/aeroportals teleport kubejs:deep_space -1000 ~ -1000`. Coordinates are absolute in the destination dimension, and each one accepts `~` or `~offset` relative to the spot the command would have picked on its own. The Y coordinate places the bottom of your airship, or your feet when no ship is detected.
- New server config option `teleport.clear_velocity_on_arrival` (default `false`). When enabled, airships arrive from any portal or command teleport with all momentum removed instead of keeping their speed.

## Fixed

- Fixed swivel bearing attachments coming loose during portal travel. A ship with an assembled swivel bearing would arrive with the attachment floating free next to the bearing, and interacting with the bearing could snap the attachment to a far-away spot. The bearing and its attachment now stay properly linked and re-lock on the other side.
- Fixed docked ships travelling through portals together sometimes losing their docking link on arrival. The connectors now find each other again on the other side.
