# AeroPortals 1.3.3

## Added

- Added optional portal-free onboard arrivals with failure feedback and automatic retry.
- Added recoverable carrier payloads for glue, decorations and attached assemblies.

## Fixed

- Fixed namespaced dimension IDs in `/aeroportals ships`.
- Fixed addon portal priorities when matching built-in portal blocks.
- Fixed ships landing inside waterlogged solid blocks.
- Fixed blocked arrivals at existing Aether and Otherside portals.
- Fixed transferred entities remaining in memory after they despawn.
- Fixed riders being left behind by AeroPortals cancelling its own teleport.
- Fixed transfers proceeding when their recovery journal cannot be written.
- Fixed portal construction overwriting bedrock and other unbreakable blocks.
- Fixed swivel bearings losing their connections during dimension travel.
- Fixed contraption diagrams being left behind during ship transfers.
- Fixed rapid AE2 spatial swaps skipping ships during portal cooldown.
- Fixed AE2 spatial containment checks overlooking rope-connected ships.
- Fixed Drive By Wire connections being deleted during ship transfers.
- Fixed Gadgets & Gizmos contraption links losing their destination endpoints.
- Fixed failed decoration restoration silently discarding recovery data.
- Fixed the declared Sable dependency range to require the supported 2.0 API.
- Fixed connected ships splitting across dimensions when a group member cannot transfer.
- Fixed cancelled or failed transfers changing destination portals, landing blocks and the End platform.
- Fixed recovery journals being removed before ship saves and source removal were verified on disk.
- Fixed restart recovery losing captured attachments or leaving duplicate decorations and glue behind.

## Changed

- Updated the license and packaged notices to require both PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 and PolyForm Perimeter 1.0.0.
- Updated server configuration and addon API documentation.
