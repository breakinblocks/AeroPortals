# AeroPortals 1.0.1

## Added

### TelePastries cakes (when TelePastries is installed)

- Right-click any TelePastries cake (Overworld, Nether, End, Twilight, Lost City, or custom configured cakes) while on your airship and the whole ship travels to that cake's dimension along with you.
- The cake slice is still consumed normally.
- End cake routes you to the standard End spawn pad. All other cakes drop the ship above the destination surface column.
