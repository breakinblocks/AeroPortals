# AeroPortals 1.3.0

## Added

- Other mods can now plug into AeroPortals. Portal blocks from other mods can be taught to carry airships, machines can be taught how to repair their own links after the trip, and packs can veto or redirect a teleport. See `docs/API.md` in the repository if you write mods.
- KubeJS support. Pack developers can register portals and machine repairs, cancel or redirect a teleport, and move ships from a script, with no addon mod needed. See `docs/KUBEJS.md` in the repository.
- Server owners can now switch off individual ways of travelling in the config, so a pack can keep Aether portals carrying airships while leaving vanilla nether and end portals alone, or any other mix. Each portal, drink, and cake has an id you list under `travel_methods.disabled`; players on foot are unaffected either way.
- New `/aeroportals methods` command lists every way of travelling on your server, its config id, and whether it is currently on.

## Fixed

- Paintings, item frames, and anything else hung on an airship now travel with it. Previously they were left floating at the spot the ship departed from and broke a moment later. This covers Immersive Paintings paintings and graffiti as well.
- A ship that reaches a portal in the same moment it is put together now has its landing spot checked properly instead of being treated as empty.
- The area a ship arrives in is now kept loaded for a few seconds. Passengers, decorations, and machines settle in properly on arrival instead of the ship being filed away the instant it lands in an area nobody is standing in.
- Create item vaults and fluid tanks no longer split back into single blocks after a trip through a portal.
- Rope pulleys, elevator pulleys, powered shafts, and drills and saws mid-break now keep working after a trip instead of losing track of what they were attached to.
- Display links no longer point at the dimension you left. A link to something on the same ship follows the ship; a link to something back in the world is left alone rather than being pointed somewhere random.
- Simulated springs, ropes, rope winches, and swivel bearing plates now keep their anchors after crossing dimensions.
- Hot air balloons are asked to rebuild themselves on arrival, so ships that fly on balloon lift keep their lift after the trip.
