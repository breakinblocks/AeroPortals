# AeroPortals 1.1.0

## Added

### Deeper and Darker portals (when Deeper and Darker is installed)

- Fly your airship into an Otherside portal and the whole ship travels to The Otherside (or back to the Overworld).
- Works the same as nether portals but with reinforced deepslate frames.
- The destination portal matches the dimensions of the source portal. If no usable portal exists at the destination, AeroPortals builds one.
