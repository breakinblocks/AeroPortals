# AeroPortals 1.3.8

## Added

- Airships can travel through Twilight Forest portal pools. Lower the ship onto a lit pool and it comes out over the linked pool on the other side, the same one a player would reach. A ship that dives into the pool comes back out climbing instead of falling (breakinblocks/AeroPortals#23).
- One-way Twilight Forest return pools do not carry ships, and when the Twilight Forest config locks the portal behind an advancement, a ship will not travel while anyone aboard is missing it.
- Added two-way ship travel through Elliemore's Deep Waters portals (breakinblocks/AeroPortals#27).

## Fixed

- Airships no longer weigh twice as much after going through a portal. The extra weight stayed until the ship was unloaded and reloaded, and came back on every trip, so ships that flew fine before a portal could sink or struggle to lift afterwards (breakinblocks/AeroPortals#24).
- Fixed completed ship transfers leaving a pending file that blocked the next trip (breakinblocks/AeroPortals#25, breakinblocks/AeroPortals#26).
