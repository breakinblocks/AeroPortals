# AeroPortals 1.2.1

## Fixed

- Fixed teleports landing at the wrong height when the destination area had not been generated or visited yet. Ships and players could arrive buried underground or floating high in the air because the terrain below them was not loaded when the landing height was picked. The destination terrain is now loaded first, so arrivals settle just above the actual surface. This affected portal arrivals at the world spawn, the teleport command, Pina Coladas, and TelePastries cakes.
- Fixed drinking a Pina Colada while not aboard an airship doing nothing. It now teleports you to the Tropics on foot, landing safely on the surface, just like it does when you are flying a ship.
