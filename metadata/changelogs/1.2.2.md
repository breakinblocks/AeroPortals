# AeroPortals 1.2.2

## Added

- Applied Energistics 2 spatial storage support. Airships caught inside a Spatial IO Port capture area now travel into the spatial storage cell along with the rest of the region, and come back out when the cell is deployed again. The ship must fit entirely inside the pylon area, or it is left behind. Docked or roped-together ships travel as a group and must all fit.
- Ships stored in a spatial cell are held in stasis: gravity is switched off inside the storage dimension, so a ship stays exactly where it was captured instead of sinking to the floor of the cell. Normal physics resumes the moment it is deployed back into the world. Modpacks can override this with their own datapack entry for the spatial storage dimension.
- Dimension stacking support via Stackable Planar Dimensions. Fly your airship down through the floor or up past the ceiling of a stacked dimension and the whole ship crosses into the adjoining dimension, arriving at the matching height with everyone aboard. The ship travels before the stacking mod can teleport riders off it individually. Ships too tall to fit between a dimension's travel boundaries stay put with a warning in the log.

## Fixed

- Fixed Create rotational blocks breaking. Rotation networks now shut down cleanly for the trip and power sources restart them on arrival, so machines spin back up on their own after a brief pause using create's own network logic.
- Fixed connected multiblocks like fluid tanks and item vaults potentially losing track of their layout when a teleport shifted the ship's stored coordinates.
- Fixed players and animals sometimes falling through the deck right after a teleport. If the ship was slow to appear on the other side, anyone aboard could slip through it and plummet. Riders are now checked for a short time after every arrival and anyone who fell below their spot on the ship is placed back where they belong, with no fall damage.
