# AeroPortals 1.2.3

## Fixed

- Fixed ships flying through a portal at speed leaving their crew behind. Everyone aboard now arrives carrying the ship's momentum instead of being dropped in stationary, so a moving ship no longer slides out from under them.
- Fixed the arrival check that puts stragglers back on deck only catching people who fell below the ship. It now catches anyone separated from the ship in any direction, follows the ship much further before giving up, and keeps watching for longer after arrival.
- Fixed players being missed by a teleport, or being pulled through a portal on their own, when the ship's motion briefly broke their footing on the deck. Anyone standing within a ship's hull is now treated as aboard it.
