# AeroPortals 1.3.4

## Fixed

- Hot air balloons keep their hot air when an airship goes through a portal, so the ship no longer loses lift and falls on arrival.
- Airships now go through a portal when their blocks actually reach it. Wide, tall or turned ships could be sent through while still several blocks away.
- Tall airships no longer get sent straight back through the portal they just came out of when they arrive next to it.
